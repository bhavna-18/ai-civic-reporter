Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Open the Report Issue page, select the appropriate civic category, provide the problem description, add the location and upload supporting evidence.

The AI verification system analyzes the uploaded evidence and generates information such as the detected issue, category, confidence, priority and summary.

After submitting a complaint, you can open My Reports or Report Tracking to view its current status and progress.

The system supports common civic problems such as potholes, garbage, water-related problems, streetlight issues and other civic concerns.
